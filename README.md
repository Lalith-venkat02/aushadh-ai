AushadhAI: Multimodal Prescription Decipherer & Dual Generic Recommender. Chosen VerticalPrimary Domain: Healthcare & Community WellnessSecondary Domain: Accessibility, Public Services & Health EquityProblem ContextIn India, over 70% of healthcare expenses are paid out-of-pocket, with medications constituting a substantial portion of recurring costs for middle- and lower-income households. While the market offers low-cost, bioequivalent generic alternatives, two major barriers prevent widespread adoption:The Legibility & Information Barrier: Over 80% of prescriptions are handwritten using proprietary brand names rather than generic chemical salt names, making it impossible for average consumers to know what molecule they are consuming or to search for alternatives.The "Trust vs. Price" Dilemma: Pure unbranded government generics (PMBJP / Jan Aushadhi) are up to 90% cheaper, but suffer from doctor skepticism and perceived quality deficits. Conversely, premium market brands charge 400%–800% markups primarily for sales and marketing overhead.2. Approach and LogicAushadhAI replaces blind pharmacy purchases with a transparent, two-tier substitution framework that balances cost reduction with clinical trust. The Dual-Recommendation LogicRather than outputting a single cheaper drug, AushadhAI evaluates medicines across two distinct consumer needs:Tier 1: Best Trusted Alternative (Quality & Availability First):Identifies identical formulations manufactured by accredited Tier-1 Indian pharmaceutical leaders (e.g., Cipla, Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy’s, Torrent). These facilities hold strict WHO-GMP and international export compliance, eliminating doctor/patient fears of substandard quality while still saving 40% to 65% compared to originator brands.Tier 2: Absolute Cheapest Option (Maximum Affordability):Maps the salt directly to the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) formulary. This provides patients managing chronic conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension) access to quality-tested medications at statutory ceiling prices, delivering up to 94% cost savings.3. How the Solution WorksStep-by-Step PipelineImage Ingestion & Preprocessing:The user snaps and uploads a photo of a doctor’s handwritten prescription or medication packaging via the responsive web interface.Multimodal Clinical Extraction (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash):The image is sent to the FastAPI backend and evaluated by gemini-1.5-flash using structured system prompts. The model performs handwriting transcription, isolates individual line items, filters non-medication text (such as doctor notes or clinic headers), and normalizes brand names into standard pharmacological chemical salts and strengths (e.g., Augmentin 625 Duo $\rightarrow$ Amoxicillin 500mg + Clavulanic Acid 125mg).Structured Schema Output:Gemini outputs strict, validated JSON containing the detected brand name, chemical salt composition, dosage form (Tablet/Capsule/Syrup), and parsing confidence.Formulary Matching & Analytics:The backend matches the normalized salt string against an indexed database of certified Indian formulations. It calculates:Baseline Expense: Prevailing retail MRP of the prescribed brand.Tier 1 Match: Best alternative brand, manufacturer, accreditation status, and percentage savings.Tier 2 Match: Government Jan Aushadhi product name, procurement unit, and maximum percentage savings.Cumulative Savings: Aggregated financial savings across the entire prescription basket.Interactive Transparency Dashboard:The frontend renders side-by-side color-coded comparative cards:Red Card: Prescribed market brand benchmark.Blue Card: Best trusted alternative with certification badges.Green Card: Jan Aushadhi option with verified savings metrics.4. Assumptions Made1. Pharmacological BioequivalenceIt is assumed that generic formulations containing the identical active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), salt form, dosage strength, and release profile (e.g., Sustained Release vs. Immediate Release) manufactured under WHO-GMP compliance provide therapeutic equivalence to originator brands.2. Pricing & Market BenchmarksBenchmark "Expensive Brand" prices reflect current Maximum Retail Prices (MRP) from major private pharmaceutical distributors in India."Cheapest" prices are indexed against published government PMBI (Pharmaceuticals & Medical Devices Bureau of India) price lists for Jan Aushadhi Kendras.Actual retail chemist discounts (e.g., 10%–15% counter discounts) may vary slightly by physical store location.3. Prescription Integrity & VerificationThe system assumes uploaded images have sufficient lighting and resolution for optical character recognition.AushadhAI acts as an informational transparency tool, not a prescribing authority. It assumes and instructs that consumers will verify substitutions with a licensed pharmacist or their consulting physician before purchasing Schedule H or X medications.4. Controlled Scope for DemonstrationFor hackathon deployment constraints (maintaining a lightweight repository footprint strictly under 10 MB), the prototype formulary indexes the top high-volume chronic and acute therapeutic categories in India (Cardiovascular, Anti-diabetic, Antibiotic, and Analgesic compounds). The architecture is designed to link directly to the complete CDSCO/NPPA national drug registry in production.5.
